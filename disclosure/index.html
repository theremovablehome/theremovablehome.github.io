Transparency

Affiliate disclosure

If a link or recommendation could earn this site money, you should be able to see that clearly.

The Removable Home is an independent guide. Some pages may contain links to online retailers or brands. If you click one of those links and later buy something, the site may earn a commission. You pay the same price you would have paid without the link.

Those links will not go live until an affiliate program — such as Amazon Associates or a network program for a wallpaper or home brand — approves this site. Until then, product mentions may appear as placeholders so the articles stay useful and can be updated without rewriting them.

FTC disclosure

This site follows the Federal Trade Commission’s guidance on endorsements and testimonials. Affiliate relationships are material connections. They are disclosed here, in the site footer, and near product recommendations in individual articles.

What does not change

A commission does not change the research standard described on the about page: damage risk, reversibility, and honest reading of manufacturer claims. If a popular product is a poor fit for renters — for example, a “removable” wallpaper that still lifts flat paint, or a hook whose listed weight assumes a perfect painted wall — the guide will say so even if that product could have paid a commission.

Amazon Associates

If this site is accepted into the Amazon Associates Program, it will earn qualifying purchases from advertising fees. Amazon and the Amazon logo are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates. The Removable Home is not Amazon.

Gifted products and sponsorships

As of the date this page was written, The Removable Home has not accepted gifted products, paid reviews, or sponsored posts. If that ever changes, the relevant article will say so in plain language at the top of the page. Sponsored content, if it exists later, will be labeled “Sponsored.”

Pricing and availability

Retail prices, stock, and product names change. Articles describe product types and the specs that matter more than a single SKU that may disappear next month. Always read the current listing, the weight or heat rating, and the removal instructions before you buy.

Placeholder links

If you click a dashed product mention and land back on this page, the retailer link has not been attached yet. That is intentional. The site will not send you through an unfinished tracking URL.

The Removable Home is an independent guide to lease-safe, no-drill apartment upgrades. It is not legal advice and is not affiliated with any landlord, property manager, or retailer.
