Privacy policy

This is a static website. At launch it does not run analytics, advertising pixels, or a comment system.

This privacy policy is for The Removable Home, a statically hosted website. It was last updated on November 10, 2026.

What this site collects

The public pages of this site are static files. They do not include a login, a comment form, a newsletter form, or third-party advertising.

The site does not currently set analytics cookies, run a tracking pixel, or embed third-party comment tools.

If you email the address listed on the contact page, the message will include whatever you choose to send (your email address and the contents of the note). That information will be used only to reply or to correct an article. It will not be sold or rented.

Hosting

The site is designed to be hosted on a free static host such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages. Those companies may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, date and time) as part of operating the service. The Removable Home does not control those infrastructure logs. Review the host’s own privacy policy if you want details:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Fonts may load from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when your browser requests those files. You can block third-party fonts in your browser if you prefer; the site remains readable with system fonts.

Affiliate programs

If retailer links are added later, clicking one will take you to a third-party site such as Amazon. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies to attribute a purchase. The Removable Home does not receive your payment details.

If a digital product is sold through Gumroad, Gumroad is the merchant of record. Gumroad processes the payment and handles sales tax where it is required. Their privacy policy applies to that checkout.

Children’s privacy

This site is written for adults making household purchasing decisions. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Future changes

If analytics, a newsletter, or advertising is added later, this page will be updated before those tools go live, including the name of the tool and the type of data it collects. A site-wide note will also be added if cookies that are not strictly technical are introduced.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
